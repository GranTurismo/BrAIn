Site APIs

BrAIn can learn how a website talks to its own backend and turn that into tools your AI agent calls directly — no clicking, no screenshots. It watches the data requests the site’s own scripts make while you use it, groups them into endpoints, and shows them here as drafts. Nothing is callable until you approve it; approved endpoints appear to agents as tools named api_<site>_<name>, and run as you, inside a tab on the site.

What stays private. Sign-in and token endpoints, analytics, and anything with a secret in its address are never learned. Tokens and CSRF headers are never stored — only their names; the values stay in the page’s memory. Sample answers are trimmed and masked (emails, tokens). Nothing is learned in Ghost mode or in private tabs. Calls that change data (or touch something sensitive) ask you every time, in the Seatbelt card.
